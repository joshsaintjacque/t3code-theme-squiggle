A theme for T3 Code

Squiggle

Light, loud and a little silly. Confetti sits in the margins until your agent starts working, then it bobs and tumbles.

What moves, and why

Confetti with a job.

Every bit of motion is tied to something the app is actually doing. If nothing is running, nothing moves.

Working

Margin confetti

Both gutters hold a Memphis collage, pastel at rest. During a turn the colours saturate while circles bob, triangles tumble and squiggles wiggle. When it ends they drain back to pastel.

Working

Header squiggle

The line under the chat header is a wave. It steps sideways for as long as the agent is busy and stops when it is done.

Background thread busy

Crawling underline

A pink squiggle crawls under any busy thread in the sidebar, and under the live Working… row, so you can see which conversations are still thinking.

Composer focus

The card pops

Click into the composer and it lifts on a longer pink shadow while a yellow dot and a blue triangle unfold from its corners.

Turn complete

Springs back

While a turn runs the composer floats on a yellow shadow. When it finishes the composer springs back to rest and the confetti fades to pastel.

Narrow windows

A slimmer margin

Under 1460px the margins slim to one calm column of shapes, and the sidebar keeps only its terrazzo so nothing sits behind thread text. Hover a thread and it leans toward you.

Watch it

A full turn, start to finish.

At rest, compose, a turn starts, a background thread goes busy, the turn completes, hover, and the narrow fallback.

Palette

One ink, five candies.

Deep indigo ink for every word, lemon for the sidebar, cobalt for actions, and pink kept for focus and work in progress. Click a row to copy its hex.

Install

Two minutes, tops.

Works on T3 Code 0.1.11 and up. The theme is one JSON file.

From the app

  1. Copy the theme JSON, or download the file.
  2. Open Settings › Appearance › Add theme.
  3. Paste it, or choose the file, and add it.
Download

From the terminal

curl -fsSL -o squiggle.json https://joshsaintjacque.github.io/t3code-theme-squiggle/squiggle.json
~/.t3/bin/t3 theme set squiggle.json
